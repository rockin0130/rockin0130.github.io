Data-Driven Global Marketing Integration

Analytics, reporting, and strategic alignment for global marketing — connecting regional initiatives to centralized KPIs and executive-ready narratives.

GitHub Selected Work

Overview

This project focused on integrating disparate marketing data sources into a coherent view for global stakeholders. The goal was to reduce manual reporting, improve consistency of metrics across regions, and surface actionable insights for campaign optimization.

What I did

Defined core metrics and data contracts, supported pipeline design for reliable ingestion, and helped translate technical outputs into dashboards and summaries that non-technical partners could trust and act on.

Outcome

A more unified story across markets — faster answers to “what’s working where,” and less time spent reconciling numbers between teams.
